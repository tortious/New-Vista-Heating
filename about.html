Our Story

Born in Denver, Built for Denver

How We Started

New Vista Heating was founded in 2005 by Marcus and Linda Reyes — two Colorado natives who believed Denver families deserved HVAC service that was honest, reliable, and fairly priced. After years working for large national chains, Marcus was frustrated by high-pressure sales tactics and cut corners. He started New Vista with a simple promise: treat every customer like a neighbor.

Where We Are Today

Over 20 years later, New Vista Heating has grown to a team of 18 certified technicians serving more than 5,000 Denver households and businesses. We're still independently owned and operated — no franchises, no call centers — just the same commitment to quality and community that Marcus and Linda started with.

Our Mission

To provide Denver families with expert, honest, and efficient HVAC service — ensuring comfort in every season while building lasting relationships grounded in trust.
